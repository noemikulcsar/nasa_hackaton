Changes made for this console:
* Handrail meshes removed, other meshes decimated (about 2.7M to 0.54M triangles).
* Textures resized to 1024 px WebP; geometry compressed with EXT_meshopt_compression.
* Node names are unchanged, so hardware such as Payload_PM, Payload_CMG2 and USLab
  can be located by name in code.
